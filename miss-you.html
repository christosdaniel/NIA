I miss you

Ivy Adwoa Nambia
Read this when you have a quiet minute

Ivy, I wanted you to have something that says it properly: I miss you.

I miss your voice, your laugh, and the way you make a whole room feel lighter. I miss the small, ordinary moments with you, because they were never ordinary.

Even when we are apart, you are the first person I think of when something good happens and the one I want beside me when things are hard.

Distance is only a place. You are my home.

Thank you for being patient, kind and loving. You make me want to be better, and you make waiting for the next time I see you worth it.

Wherever you are tonight, know that someone is thinking about you, smiling about you and counting down until he sees you again.

See you soon, my love.

Until then, hold on to this: you are loved, and you are missed.

Yours, Daniel
